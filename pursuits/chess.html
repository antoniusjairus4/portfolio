Calculation & Endurance

Chess is mentally demanding. Unlike physical sports, there is no athleticism to rely on — it demands pure calculation, positional evaluation, and calculation under severe time pressure. Every move tests how far ahead you can calculate while managing uncertainty.

Style & Openings

Plays the Grünfeld Defence as Black — an aggressive, theory-heavy opening that invites White to construct a pawn center and then systematically dismantles it. As White, favors the Nimzowitsch Opening — unconventional, provocative, and designed to take opponents out of memorized lines early. Organized department-level chess tournaments at SKCET.
