# Phase 4 — NeuroShield Greybox

## Research narrative

NeuroShield is presented as an ongoing six-person machine-learning research effort exploring patterns in Alzheimer's-related imaging data. Antonius's role is stated as one contributor training models with ADNI datasets and producing results, graphs, and experimental evidence for team analysis. The chapter follows **Observation → Hypothesis → Experiment → Evidence → Uncertainty** and ends with more informed structure rather than a definitive answer.

## Factual claim boundary

The semantic copy does not describe NeuroShield as a detector, diagnosis system, medical device, clinically validated product, or finished clinical tool. A concise disclosure states that the work is experimental research and is not a replacement for professional medical judgment. No accuracy values, model counts, disease-stage claims, patient records, or clinical outcomes are displayed.

The repository contains conflicting legacy language: `src/content/ventures.ts` calls NeuroShield an early Alzheimer's detection system, the former interactive demo uses a heatmap-confidence presentation, and older documentation also uses detection language. The locked Experience Bible instead defines an ongoing team research project. Phase 4 follows that conservative framing. The repository also uses both NeuroShield and NeuroScan AI; this chapter retains NeuroShield because it is the current public project name and destination.

## Spatial concept

The world is an authored research space made from evidence planes, nodes, connections, branches, gaps, terminated paths, and negative space. It contains no brain model, medical scanner, dashboard, patient record, particle cloud, or random network. The opening retains one restrained abstract relationship from Palindrome; the relationship develops gaps and competing branches as NeuroShield takes ownership.

## Five beats

1. **Observation** — two evidence planes and a measured but incomplete relationship establish that data exists before meaning is known.
2. **Hypothesis** — selected observations gain multiple conditional connections and the initial relationship opens into branches.
3. **Experiment** — run-like structures compare paths; a secondary path and one visibly terminated path appear.
4. **Evidence** — more planes, nodes, supporting paths, competing paths, and depth enter the composition without producing a single answer.
5. **Uncertainty** — the richest frame remains disciplined and legible while gaps, alternatives, and empty space keep it explicitly unfinished.

All transformations derive from normalized chapter progress. Reverse movement and direct jumps reconstruct the same state without completion callbacks or direction-dependent animation state.

## Evidence and uncertainty representation

Evidence uses paper-like planes with restrained line marks rather than fabricated clinical records. Neutral metadata uses abstract labels such as `OBS / A`, `EXP / 02`, and `RUN / B`. Primary paths are structurally heavier than secondary paths. A terminated branch uses both a line endpoint and a rare crimson cap, so its meaning does not depend on color alone. Gaps and absent endpoints represent unresolved evidence through absence.

## Camera strategy

Five authored camera positions provide measured investigative movement: a slight lateral examination, restrained movement toward the experimental structure, and a final reframe that exposes competing context. Desktop and portrait use separate camera paths and fields of view. There is no orbit control, dramatic fly-through, spin, or continuous scanner motion.

## Palindrome to NeuroShield handoff

Palindrome's room, human, household objects, financial fragments, and typography retire with that scene. One abstract measured relationship begins NeuroShield. Its center gap widens and secondary branches emerge, turning an organized relationship into a question. The shared renderer switches scene ownership once at the chapter boundary; no financial object is transformed into a medical object.

## Typography and semantics

The chapter uses the portfolio's modern grotesk for the project title and conceptual statements, and mono for abstract research metadata. Cinzel Decorative is not used. Essential project identity, team context, contribution, disclosure, and destination live in semantic HTML rather than inside WebGL.

## Renderer lifecycle

NeuroShield registers with the existing `ProjectExperienceProvider`. The provider selects one active project scene among KaiForge, Palindrome, and NeuroShield and mounts it inside the single shared React Three Fiber canvas. Rendering remains demand-driven, controller updates invalidate the frame, DPR remains capped by the existing quality tier, and inactive scene resources are disposed through declarative unmounting.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop uses a broad layered evidence field with edge-positioned editorial copy. Portrait uses its own camera path, wider field of view, compressed structure scale, reduced metadata, and bottom-positioned beat copy. Both preserve the five-beat narrative and intentional negative space without horizontal overflow.

## Reduced motion and no WebGL

Reduced motion collapses the chapter into a complete static editorial composition with evidence planes, nodes, incomplete relationships, project identity, team context, disclosure, and destination. The no-WebGL path uses the same semantic DOM/CSS composition. Neither fallback mounts the project canvas or displays a renderer error.

## Performance findings

The greybox uses eight evidence planes, ten nodes, nine connection groups, primitive geometry, basic/standard materials, fog, and three restrained lights. It adds no textures, models, shaders, particles, physics, post-processing, external assets, or continuous animation loop. The existing demand renderer and DPR cap remain in effect.
