# Phase 6 — Achievements Kinetic Editorial Greybox

## Editorial concept

Achievements is a semantic DOM/CSS performance in which spatial travel becomes typographic authority. It contains four distinct discipline sections rather than a gallery, card system, dashboard, or WebGL scene. Each section has one dominant motion: trajectory, impact, calculation, or scale.

## Skills to Achievements handoff

The final Skills axis meets a matching two-dimensional rule at the top of Achievements. Skills depth, category labels, geometry, and WebGL ownership retire. The rule becomes Table Tennis trajectory punctuation before typography assumes control.

## Four discipline identities

- **Table Tennis:** a small DOM ball and one rule translate scroll into a restrained rally path. `2×` and `STATE CHAMPION` resolve as the dominant mass.
- **Karate:** `BLACK` and `BELT` approach from opposite sides, lock into alignment, and stop. One brief crimson rule marks impact; the resolved composition remains still.
- **Chess:** a restrained grid appears while Rapid, Blitz, and Bullet ratings move into place through three discrete decisions.
- **Public Speaking / Leadership:** `15+ SUCCESSFUL SPEECHES` establishes presence, retires, and gives `15,000+ AUDIENCE` a fully legible resolving moment before it expands toward the portrait edges. The composition then subtracts itself into parchment silence.

## Typography and rule system

Clash Display, the existing modern grotesk, carries monumental statements and numbers. JetBrains Mono carries indexes, motion principles, rating labels, and the temporary publication note. The inherited rule changes function per discipline: trajectory, impact, grid alignment, baseline, then quiet neutral edge.

## Deterministic choreography

Each discipline owns a normal document section with a local sticky stage. Its state is derived directly from the section's current progress through the shared chapter-progress controller. No completion callbacks, accumulated transforms, wheel interception, nested scrolling, secondary smooth-scroll instance, or continuous animation loop is used. Reverse scroll and direct jumps reconstruct from current position.

## Desktop and portrait composition

Desktop uses edge contact, controlled crop, wide directional movement, and deliberate negative space. Portrait changes line breaks and rhythm: Table Tennis stacks its achievement, Karate separates vertically with the impact rule below both words, Chess ratings stack beneath the heading, and the audience number resolves in full before approaching the viewport edges. The document never scrolls horizontally.

## Reduced motion and no WebGL

Reduced motion removes long scroll travel and renders four static editorial compositions with the same semantic claims. The audience count is sized to remain fully legible in the static desktop and portrait layouts. Achievements has no canvas or renderer dependency. Forced WebGL failure in earlier chapters leaves it fully operational.

## Accessibility

The chapter has one semantic `h2`, four discipline `h3` headings, a description list for ratings, and real text for every claim. The ball, trajectory, grid, impact rule, baseline, inherited rule, and silence rule are decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Visual duplicates of achievement claims are not created.

## Performance and renderer shutdown

The chapter uses transform and opacity updates on a small, fixed DOM structure. It adds no canvas, WebGL, images, filters, physics, particles, per-character splitting, or ambient RAF. The global Ether layer is disabled while Achievements approaches or owns the viewport, and the shared project renderer has already released before the chapter begins.

## Old Achievements implementation

`DisciplinesScrollSection` is no longer mounted. Its LiquidEther, CursorGrid, photography, navigation dots, count-up callbacks, and single master pin therefore do not run. The older discipline-scroll component, modal/template implementation, content schema, photo pipeline, assets, and legacy tests remain cleanup candidates until removal is reviewed explicitly.

## Achievements to Contact contract

After the 15,000+ peak, the audience composition fades, rules retire, crimson is absent, and a warm parchment field becomes dominant with one quiet neutral baseline. No sport, Karate, Chess, WebGL, or achievement-number imagery survives. Contact can enter through whitespace and near-native scrolling.
