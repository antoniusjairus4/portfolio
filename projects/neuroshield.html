NeuroShield AI

Multimodal Deep Learning System for Early Alzheimer's Disease Risk Prediction & MCI-to-AD Conversion Trajectory Modeling using the ADNI Dataset.

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

Addressing a 55M+ Global Burden

Alzheimer's disease (AD) is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder affecting over 55 million people globally, projected to reach 153 million by 2050. Pathological changes—specifically amyloid-beta plaques and hyperphosphorylated tau tangles—begin silently 15–20 years before clinical memory loss appears.

NeuroShield AI is a novel multimodal deep learning system engineered to predict Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) to Alzheimer's conversion timelines. By fusing 3D structural MRI, PET neuroimaging (AV45/AV1451), and plasma blood biomarkers (p-tau181, p-tau217, Aβ42/40), NeuroShield AI provides time-resolved risk curves (12, 24, 36, 48 months).

2. KEY CLINICAL INNOVATIONS

Disambiguating Atrophy & Missing Modality Tolerance

Depression vs. Alzheimer's Confound: Hippocampal volume loss occurs in both Alzheimer's and Clinical Depression. Standard computer vision models often misclassify depression as early AD. NeuroShield AI solves this by integrating plasma blood biomarkers (p-tau181 & Aβ42/40 ratio) with FT-Transformers, explicitly isolating true neurodegenerative signals from psychiatric confounds.

Clinically-Staged Missing-Modality Tolerance: Expensive PET scans are rarely available at GP triage. Trained with random modality dropout, NeuroShield AI operates gracefully across 3 clinical stages—from blood-only screening to blood+MRI confirmation and full 3-modality confirmation.

3. ADNI DATASET & PREPROCESSING PIPELINE

Rigorous Neuroimaging Harmonization

Built on the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI 1/GO/2/3) dataset across 2,000+ participants with longitudinal follow-ups up to 48 months.

MRI PIPELINE

Structural T1-Weighted MRI

DICOM to NIfTI via dcm2niix → N4ITK bias field correction → Deep learning skull stripping (HD-BET) → Diffeomorphic registration to MNI152 space (ANTs SyNQuick) → FastSurfer ROI extraction (Hippocampus, Entorhinal cortex, Amygdala).

PET PIPELINE

AV45 Amyloid & AV1451 Tau PET

Rigid 6-DOF PET-to-MRI co-registration → Spatial warp to MNI space → SUVr normalization using whole/inferior cerebellar grey matter → Partial volume correction (GTM method) → ComBat site harmonization.

PLASMA BIOMARKERS

Blood & Tabular Features

Extraction of p-tau181, p-tau217, Aβ42/40 ratio, NfL, and GFAP → MICE chained-equation imputation → neuroCombat lab batch harmonization → Longitudinal rate of change (slope) trajectory engineering.
